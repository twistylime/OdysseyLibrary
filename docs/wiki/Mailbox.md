# Mailbox

Every reward from every Odyssey mod (and anything returned from a menu) goes to your mailbox, never
straight into your inventory, so nothing is lost when your inventory is full or you are offline.

- Mail never expires.
- You get a clickable chat message and a title when new mail arrives, and a reminder when you log in.
- Claim one mail by clicking it, or everything with **Claim all**. Items that don't fit stay in the mail.
- Points, XP and special rewards in a mail are given when you claim it.
- **Soulbound items** (e.g. custom-origin reward items) belong to one player. If one is dropped, picked up
  by someone else, destroyed or ends up in another player's inventory, it returns to its owner's mailbox.
